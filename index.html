        Transfer or card payment

        On that date the amount comes out of the account you pick and comes off the card balance. Future dates apply by themselves when the day arrives.

        Commission pay date

        Pick the paycheck your commission is paid with. It shows on the calendar as its own line on that day every month, even in months with an extra paycheck. Each commission can also pick a different paycheck or a specific date when you add or edit it.

        Charity donations

        Optional. Track what you give to charity each month, as a percent of what comes in or a set dollar amount. It gets its own section on the Budget page so you can plan around it.

        Before you start

        • Your information stays private. Other users cannot see your budget, and we do not look at the numbers, names or notes you enter. It is stored for you with our cloud providers, and we only access it to keep the app working, to help you when you ask, or if the law requires it.
        • It is a planning tool, not advice. Totals and balances are estimates based on what you type in and may differ from your bank or card statements. Nothing here is financial, tax or legal advice.
        • Not connected to your bank. The app does not log in to your bank or card accounts.
        • Keep your password safe. You are responsible for what happens under your account.
